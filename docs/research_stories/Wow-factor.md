## Research
The purpose of this research is to find out what impresses people regarding robots so we can make a more appealing product for our target audience.
To find the right factors, we made a survey which helped us determine what others consider to be a “wow” factor in a robot. We first asked them what their opinion is about robots and what is cool about them. 
To get a little bit more information from the interviewed participants we asked them what a robot needs to do to get them impressed, and if a robot ever left an impression behind.

## Results
#### What do they think of robots?

The interviewed students have a mixed opinion about robots. Some of them find robots interesting and useful, while others consider them scary and think they will affect their life and career.

#### What do they find cool about robots?

The opinions of the students are quite divided again. Their general opinion about robots is that they find it cool how robots can do the same thing and even more than people can do, which makes life easier for us.

#### What makes them go “wow” regarding robots?
This question has yet again quite some interesting answers. Two students said they were impressed when a robot starts dancing, while other students said they drop their jaws when a robot is able to fight, flip, be self conscious or do something which a human is not able to do.

#### What does a robot need to do to get the students impressed?

The answers for this question are quite interesting to say the least. One of the students answered the question with “the robot should do something which I couldn’t with my own body, or more efficiently compared to my body ”. Another student said he would be impressed by the robot if it starts peeing beer, possibly referring to a robot dog from boston dynamics which learns to pee beer in a cup. Some other answers from students include a robot being able to do two flips, a robot which would be able to twerk, doing household duties and if the robot Bender from TV Show Futurama comes to life.

#### Has a robot ever left an impression behind by the interviewed students?

The final question has been answered quite honestly by the students. Two talked about their experience with a waiter robot; one of them collided with a waiter robot and was surprised by the fact the robot recognised what happened and apologized. The other student claimed they were afraid to get eaten by it if they did not thank the waiter bot.
Another student referred to the beer peeing dog they saw on youtube, confirming they were indeed referring to the boston dynamics dog which learned to pee in a cup, claiming they were both impressed and surprised by the accuracy of the robot. Another interviewed student referred to “The Da Vinci Surgical System”, a precise robot which is able to operate on the human body without having the risk of human error and increase the chance of survival of the patient.
The last student said they were both impressed and scared by a robot expressing human traits.

 

## Conclusion
It seems the greatest wow factor among students is around the physical tasks/motions of a robot. If the robot is able to do precise actions which require movement, we can leave a greater impression behind in comparison to a robot looking cool and slick.
