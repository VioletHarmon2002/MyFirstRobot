## Current motors
The current motor of the original Little Endian 2.0 is a 28BYJ-48 - 5V stepper motor. The torque of the stepper motor is 600 gf/cm, equalling 0.6 kilogram-force centimeter (kgf/cm) using 5 volts.

## Potential motors
SG-90 360 degree continuous servo motor
The SG-90 continuous servo motor is a small servo with 1.3kg/cm and 1.5kg/cm torque using 4.8 and 6 volts respectively.
