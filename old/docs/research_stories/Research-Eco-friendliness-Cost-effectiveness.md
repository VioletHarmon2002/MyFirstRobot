# Research balance between eco friendliness and cost effectiveness
April 26, 2024

Written by: Timo Lambregts

## Overview
We want to make the Little Endian 2.0 friendly towards the environment while also maintaining a low budget. 

## What we want to accomplsh
1. Our primary goal is to keep the Little Endian as inexpensive as possible. 
2. Our secondary goal is to make the product as environmentally friendly as we can.

## Discoveries
### 100% recycled plexiglass
According to [kunststofplaten.nl](https://kunststofplatenshop.nl/product/plexiglas-wit-3mm/), they sell 60cmx120cm 3mm thick plexiglass which they claim to be 100% recycled. The price would be 42.33 euro’s incl. VAT.

### Really inexpensive
During my research, I discovered I can buy a 122cmx61cm 3.6mm thick multiplex plate for 5 euros from [Praxis](https://www.praxis.nl/bouwmaterialen/hout/multiplex/multiplex-hardhout-122x61cm-3-6mm/5661362). The cost of multiplex is ⅛ of the recycled plexiglass, which is a huge difference.

## Conclusion
Due to a huge price difference, it would be in our best interest to use multiplex to make our project significantly cheaper.
