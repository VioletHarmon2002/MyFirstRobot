# Eco friendly parts
April 25, 2024

Written by: Jayden van Oorschot

## Robot dog (B)
- Plexiglas/acrylic: 100% recyclable, versatile, very durable, costs less energy to produce than most materials and lightweight so transport costs less.
- Multiplex: depends on the source of the wood, but biodegradable
- PCB: Depends on manufacturer, I can’t find info on PCBway but Eurocircuits is based in Germany and Belgium where rules are strict
- Microcontroller/MCU: has to be RoHS compliant
- SG90 servo: If it’s made by a specific brand, like TowerPro,it can be RoHS compliant. The one we already have has no brand name or logo on it, and I don’t know if it’s RoHS compliant. Other servos are also an option.
- Sensors and LEDs must be RoHS compliant

| Part | Description | Eco-friendly? |
|---|---|---|
| Acrylic | 100% recyclable, versatile, very durable, costs less energy to produce than most materials and lightweight so transport costs less. | Yes |
| Multiplex | Depends on the source of the wood, but biodegradable | Yes (if source is good) |
| PCB | Depends on manufacturer, I can’t find info on PCBway but Eurocircuits is based in Germany and Belgium where rules are strict. However, manufacturing a PCB is an energy-intensive and high emission process that involves fiberglass, copper and other bad things. | Probably not |
| MCU | Has to be RoHS compliant, but the manufacturing of microchips is always bad for the environment | |
| Servo | If it’s made by a specific brand, like [TowerPro](https://www.towerpro.com.tw/product/sg90-7/),it can be RoHS compliant. The one we already have has no brand name or logo on it, and I don’t know if it’s RoHS compliant. Other servos are also an option. | yes, depending on manufacturer |
